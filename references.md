## Thomas C. Hales and the earlier motivic-measure background

13. **Thomas C. Hales**, “What is motivic measure?”, *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society* **42** (2005), no. 2, 119–135. [arXiv:math/0312229](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0312229), submitted 2003. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-05-01053-0). [Readable PDF](https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~francois.loeser/bams.pdf).

Hales's article is an exposition of arithmetic motivic measure as developed by **Jan Denef** and **François Loeser**, drawing on earlier motivic-integration ideas of **Maxim Kontsevich**. Its arXiv version includes historical commentary by **Michael D. Fried**. The authors of the original results must receive credit separately from the expositor.

## Additional foundational names appearing in these notes

- **Max Dehn** — the non-volume scissors invariant addressing Hilbert's third problem.
- **Friedhelm Waldhausen** — Waldhausen categories and algebraic K-theory.
- **Daniel Quillen** — foundational algebraic K-theory, localization/dévissage analogies.
- **Alexander Goncharov** — conjectural connection of polytope scissors groups, Dehn complexes, and algebraic K-theory.
- **Michael Larsen**, **Valery Lunts**, **Lev Borisov** — results/questions around Grothendieck rings, stable birational invariants, and Lefschetz zero divisors.
